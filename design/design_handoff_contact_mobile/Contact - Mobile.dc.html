4a — recommended direction. Contact details come first, before the form: four 64px tappable rows with chevrons, so calling, emailing or getting directions is one tap from the top of the page. Hero compresses to a 180px band with the copy on white. Form keeps the desktop's paired name row, then one field per row, with 52px inputs and a full-width submit. Hours and the emergency card stack below the form, and the sidebar's portrait CTA becomes a full-bleed band above the footer.
Order matters here

Desktop reads left-to-right: details in the sidebar, form in the main column. At mw-880 the sidebar stacks first — which is correct and worth keeping. Most phone visitors to a contact page want to call, not type.

What changes is the treatment: the four info rows are currently 40px icon + two text lines with no tap affordance, sitting at about 56px each. As 64px rows with chevrons they read as actions.

Flagged, not changed

The address on this page (2911 Capital Ave SW, Battle Creek) differs from the address in the TopBar, Footer and Home's ReachUs section (8145 Valleywood Lane, Portage). contact.data.ts documents this as intentional, but on a 390px screen the footer's Portage address sits about one screen below the Battle Creek one, which reads as an error.

The hours card says weekends are 12:00–6:00 while the utility strip says 12–8 PM every day. Same conflict, now visible in one scroll.

ContactHours uses <Link to="#hours"> for an in-page anchor; that should be a plain <a href> or it re-routes.
