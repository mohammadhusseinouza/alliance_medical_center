Why this page is different

No sidebar, no breadcrumb, nine full-width sections — this is a landing page for employers, not a service detail page. Nothing from §15–20 applies except the footer and the action bar.

Two problems the current mobile output has:

  • The hero reserves pt-[168px] at mw-650 so the glass card clears the photo. At 390px that is a screen and a half before the first heading, and the card's backdrop-blur over a busy crop makes the body copy hard to read.
  • Four service cards, six exam cards, three drug-test cards and three additional-service cards all collapse to one column, which is roughly 3,600px of stacked cards.
Flagged, not changed

This page uses an 18px gutter (mw-650:px-[18px]) while every other page uses 16px. Worth picking one.

The two xl-only floating accent chips ("Safer Workplaces", "Employee Wellness") never appear below 1280px, so mobile loses them entirely. That's fine — flagging it in case they were meant as key messaging.
